About StudentHub

StudentHub is an academic portal engineered to bridge communication gaps among university students, faculty members, and administrative staff. Designed with accessibility, responsive layout architecture, and enterprise security in mind, the platform serves as a single source of truth for daily campus operations.

Our Vision

To foster an inclusive, digitally integrated academic ecosystem where students can focus on learning, skill acquisition, and co-curricular achievements with minimal administrative friction.

Core Technology Principles

Built following modern web development standards including semantic HTML5 elements, CSS Grid and Flexbox responsiveness, client-side and server-side data validations, RESTful JSON APIs, and secure RBAC sessions.

Academic Information

Developed under the Web Development Frameworks (ITUE203) curriculum at the Faculty of Technology and Engineering (FTE), Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT).
